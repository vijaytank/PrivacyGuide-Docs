## Introduction

At **Privacy Guide**, your privacy is our only priority. This app is built to provide transparency and education on Android permissions. We are committed to protecting any personal information you may have on your device and ensuring a safe user experience.

## Data Collection and Usage

**Privacy Guide does NOT collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII) or sensitive user data.**

- **On-Device Processing**: All permission audits, risk calculations, and activity tracking occur strictly on your device. No data is ever uploaded to a remote server.
- **Permission Auditing**: The app requires the `QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES` permission to identify which apps are installed on your device. This data is used solely to generate your Privacy Score and provide custom auditing. This list of apps never leaves your device.
- **Access Tracker**: The app uses `AppOpsManager` to log when other apps access sensitive permissions (like Camera or Microphone). This log is stored in your device's private storage and is never shared.
- **No Analytics/Tracking**: We do not use third-party analytics (like Google Analytics) or advertising SDKs.

## Third-Party Access

We do not share any data with third parties. Your device's privacy state is for your eyes only.

## Your Controls

Since all processing is local, you have full control over your data:
- **Revoking Permissions**: You can revoke Privacy Guide's permissions at any time through Android Settings.
- **Clearing Data**: Clearing the app's cache and data via Android Settings permanently removes all local logs and scoring history.
