## Full Description

**Take control of your digital footprint with Privacy Guide.**

Privacy Guide is a powerful, offline-first privacy assistant designed for everyday users. Have you ever wondered why a simple app needs access to your camera or microphone? Privacy Guide explains it all in plain English.

### Key Features

*   🛡️ **Privacy Audit Dashboard**: Get a comprehensive overview of your device's privacy health and see which apps have access to sensitive permissions.
*   📊 **Privacy Health Score**: Our advanced risk heuristics calculate a "Privacy Score" for your device, highlighting apps that need your attention.
*   🧠 **Risk Explanations**: Understand the dangers of combined permissions (e.g., Camera + Location) with detailed, easy-to-read reasoning.
*   🕰️ **Real-Time Access Tracker**: View a chronological log of when apps access your data.
*   📚 **Data Safety Education Center**: Learn about Android's data safety labels through our library of 15+ interactive guides.
*   📍 **Privacy-First Design**: No online sync, no data collection, and no third-party trackers. All processing happens 100% on your device.

### How it Works

Privacy Guide uses official Android APIs (AppOpsManager) to track permission usage and audits your installed app inventory to provide localized privacy insights. Unlike other tools, we never upload your data, and we don't require intrusive permissions to keep you safe.

**Download Privacy Guide today and start your journey toward a more transparent and secure mobile experience.**
